ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

Our 15-Point Construction Standard

We adhere to rigorous civil engineering codes (IS 456, IS 1893, IS 875), verified raw materials, and documented milestone audits across every stage of construction.

01

Soil Investigation & Planning

  • Soil test & bearing capacity
  • Site analysis & planning
  • Structural design based on site
  • Architect-designed plans
IS 1893 SBC Tested
02

Quality Materials

  • Cement: UltraTech
  • Steel: JSW / Tata / VIZAG
  • Sand: Quality river sand
  • Aggregates: Proper grading
  • Bricks/Blocks: Tested quality
UltraTech Tata / JSW
03

Foundation & Footings

  • Proper excavation to hard strata
  • PCC and reinforcement bed
  • Footing construction as per design
  • Plinth beam as per structural design
  • Waterproofing where required
Heavy Plinth Waterproof DPC
04

Columns & Structure

  • Reinforcement as per design
  • Proper bar bending and placement
  • Quality shuttering for smooth finish
  • Concrete compaction and vibration
  • Load distribution as per structural design
IS 456 Compliant Pin Vibration
05

Slabs & Beams

  • Slab thickness as per design
  • Top & bottom reinforcement
  • Proper cover blocks for protection
  • Electrical & plumbing provisions
  • Quality concrete and curing
Cover Blocks Curing Control
06

Testing & Quality Checks

  • Concrete cube testing (7 & 28 days)
  • Sand, aggregate and brick tests
  • Material quality verification
  • Slab, beam and column checks
  • Documented quality records
Cube Tested Verified Log
07

Masonry & Walls

  • Quality bricks/blocks
  • Correct mortar mix ratio
  • Proper alignment and bonding
  • Lintel and openings as per design
  • Curing and plastering (2 coats)
RCC Lintels Dual Plaster
08

Architectural Design

  • Designed by Studio Zaxis, Hyderabad
  • Functional floor plans (zero waste)
  • Better natural ventilation
  • More daylight and open spaces
  • Vastu-compliant design options
Studio Zaxis Vastu Aligned
09

Ventilation & Natural Light

  • Cross ventilation in all spaces
  • Large windows for illumination
  • Air flow between rooms
  • Natural light in all rooms
  • Healthy and comfortable living
Eco Daylight Cross Breeze
10

Electrical Systems

  • Polycab wires (FR Grade copper)
  • Legrand switches modular range
  • Proper electrical point planning
  • Earthing with RCCB/MCB
  • Transformer with sufficient load
  • Lightning protection & electric fencing
Polycab Legrand RCCB Safe
11

Plumbing & Drainage

  • Quality water supply CPVC/UPVC pipes
  • Proper pipe sizing and routing
  • Hot & cold water provisions
  • Correct drainage slopes & traps
  • Leak-proof jointing (pressure tested)
  • Terrace and rainwater drainage
Hydro Tested Leak Proof
12

Bathrooms & Sanitaryware

  • Cera / Parryware sanitaryware
  • Jaquar taps and CP fittings
  • Anti-skid tiles on all floors
  • Proper slope and drainage
  • Waterproofing and ventilation
  • Modern and durable finish
Jaquar Cera Anti-Skid
13

Wall Finish & Paints

  • Birla wall putty (2 coats)
  • Asian Paints / Birla Opus
  • Smooth surface preparation
  • Premium quality satin finish
  • Wide range of colour options
Birla Putty Asian Paints
14

Parking & Common Areas

  • Spacious parking for cars & bikes
  • Easy vehicle movement & turning
  • Adequate lighting with sensors
  • Pedestrian safety demarcations
  • Well-planned common areas
VDF Flooring Wide Turning
15

Power Backup & Safety

  • Kirloskar generators silent acoustic DG
  • Adequate power backup for lifts & common
  • Earthing and lightning protection
  • Electrical safety systems
  • Security systems and fencing
Kirloskar DG Auto Switch
